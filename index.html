Proxies and limitations (read this)

  • Flat curves for €STR and EURIBOR 6M, until the bootstrapped curves of project 09 are plugged in.
  • Synthetic swaption vols (no free source): vols ±20% → CVA +15.7% / −15.5%.
  • Synthetic CDS spreads: spreads ±25% → CVA +22.6% / −23.5%.
  • Historical σ (P measure) instead of implied σ: CVA −23.5%.
  • One-factor model: slope risk is nearly invisible (rejected in the point-in-time backtest).
  • MTA added to the threshold, cash flows during the MPoR ignored. Details in the validation report.
